# Inert example bank

Use reserved `.example` and `.test` domains. Avoid operational addresses/phone numbers. All messages are fictional course copy, not actual intercepted communications.

## E1: supplier email (prototype)
From display: Nadia · Maju Supplies. Address: nadia@maju-supplies.example.
Subject: Re: Invoice MS-1048.
Body: Hi Farid, our bank details have changed. Please use the revised details for today's payment. Please complete this before 5 PM. The amount stays the same. Thanks, Nadia.
Attachment card: Updated-payment-details.pdf. No download.
Inspection destination: https://files.maju-supplies.example/MS-1048. Inert text.

## E6: genuine support
Mei: I'm following up on ticket DEMO-204 that you raised. Please check the details in our normal support portal before we proceed.
Known portal confirms ticket and technician. Access still follows policy. Do not portray this as an actual customer's portal.

## E7: QR notification
Account access notice: Your account requires a review. Scan to continue.
QR tile: illustrative square with text 'Demo QR / no scan'. A reveal shows an inert destination. Do not use a functional linking/authentication QR.

## E8: fake verification/repair
A static panel says: Verification requires an additional device action.
Next reveal: a supposed fix asks the person to open an operating-system tool. Show a 'Stop and contact approved support' explanation. Never print a runnable instruction or command string.

## E9: genuine HR request
Verified HR contact asks for staff records for an authorised task. The participant must check the approved sharing route and minimum information required. This example is legitimate; verification alone does not replace authorisation.

## E10: callback notice
An unexpected subscription notice lists DEMO-NUMBER. Ask participants to check the known service account/support rather than the supplied callback contact. No live number.

Optional example vocabulary can vary by audience: documents, procurement, invoices, benefits, meeting invites, shared folders. Keep the safety decision and source attribution unchanged. No actual customer or partner impersonation is needed.
