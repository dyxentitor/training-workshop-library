# Claude final deliverables

Produce:
1. `dist/workshop.html`: complete offline deck with all screens, notes, interactions, module navigation and references.
2. `dist/participant-quick-reference.html`: print-friendly card adapted only to confirmed configuration. PDF export may be added if rendered and checked.
3. `dist/facilitator-guide.md` (or checked print HTML): complete runbook and notes.
4. `dist/README.md`: open/present instructions, same-screen note warning, configuration/build instructions and remaining customer inputs.
5. `dist/SOURCE_INDEX.md`: cited primary sources and recheck status.
6. `validation/QA_REPORT.md`: performed checks, evidence, limitations, coverage and changes to slide counts/timing.
7. `validation/screenshots/`: all slide references plus important reveal states, named by slide ID/viewport/state.
8. Editable source/data/config and `build` instructions if final HTML is generated from separate files.
9. One final ZIP containing the deliverables and necessary editable source, excluding dependencies, caches, secrets and scratch artefacts.

This contract is a specification. These final files do not yet exist in this package. Do not count empty placeholders as deliverables or claim the full deck was tested from prototype tests alone. Live hosting, emails, campaign sends and account access are not part of this build.
