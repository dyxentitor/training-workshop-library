# M2 content script

## SL16: An urgent request from the manager
Time: 11:15–11:20 (5 minutes). Layout: chat. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Notice authority pressure

Screen copy:
- The deadline is close.
- The normal approval process still applies.

Evidence to create: Ravi’s display name requests an exceptional purchase.
Interaction: Reveal request and secrecy instruction.
Reveal: Confirm through established work channels.
Facilitator notes: Connect to opening without replaying its six slides.
Sources: S7, S8

## SL17: What the pressure asks you to skip
Time: 11:20–11:27 (7 minutes). Layout: annotated. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Identify process bypass

Screen copy:
- Urgency
- Secrecy
- An exception to normal approval

Evidence to create: Fictional message: “Handle this before the meeting; I’ll approve later.”
Interaction: Identify the business check being bypassed.
Reveal: The risk is the requested exception, even if the person is genuine.
Facilitator notes: Discuss authority, fear and helpfulness without stereotyping victims.
Sources: S7

## SL18: A polite way to pause
Time: 11:27–11:33 (6 minutes). Layout: roleplay. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Practise a usable response

Screen copy:
- “I can help once I confirm through our normal approval process.”

Evidence to create: Pairs act as employee and impatient requester.
Interaction: Two-minute roleplay, swap, debrief.
Reveal: Choose a specific verification route and next step.
Facilitator notes: Make room for cultural/authority concerns. Managers should support this behaviour.
Sources: S20

## SL19: The voice on the phone
Time: 11:33–11:40 (7 minutes). Layout: voice. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Recognise information-rich caller impersonation

Screen copy:
- A caller knows your name and manager.
- They ask for an account reset or code.

Evidence to create: Scripted caller claims IT support.
Interaction: Read a captioned script; ask what proves identity.
Reveal: Call the established service desk.
Facilitator notes: No voice cloning of real people required. Do not collect codes.
Sources: S8

## SL20: A face or voice cannot approve a payment
Time: 11:40–11:48 (8 minutes). Layout: case. Evidence: guidance.
Objective: Use business controls for synthetic-media risk

Screen copy:
- Verify the transaction through a separate authorised process.

Evidence to create: Reference the Hong Kong case from opening plus a fictional manager call.
Interaction: Compare cosmetic detection with independent verification.
Reveal: Visual glitches are insufficient as the only defence.
Facilitator notes: Do not claim all synthetic media is detectable or all video calls are unsafe.
Sources: S9, S7

## SL21: Which number will you call?
Time: 11:48–11:55 (7 minutes). Layout: decision. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Choose existing contact records

Screen copy:
- The email includes a new phone number to confirm new bank details.

Evidence to create: Old vendor record, new email signature and directory contact.
Interaction: Choose callback destination, explain rationale.
Reveal: Use established records; do not rely on a number supplied with the change.
Facilitator notes: The genuine vendor may have a new number too; update records through approved procedure.
Sources: S7

Decision options:
- New number in the email: It comes from the same unverified request. 
- Existing vendor record: Use the established contact and required approval. [Preferred]
- Wait without telling anyone: Hold the action and notify the appropriate owner. 

## SL22: Keep the process when the request is genuine
Time: 11:55–12:00 (5 minutes). Layout: decision. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Avoid labelling all sensitive requests as fraud

Screen copy:
- A verified vendor really has changed banks.

Evidence to create: Established contact confirms change; approval is still pending.
Interaction: Ask whether verification alone permits payment.
Reveal: Complete authorised approval and record updates.
Facilitator notes: Explicit legitimate example. Verification and authorisation serve different purposes.
Sources: S7


### SL22 explicit decision key
- Pay immediately after the call: Identity verification does not replace payment authorisation.
- Complete the approved bank-change and payment process: Proceed only after verification, records and approvals are complete. [Preferred]
- Treat every bank change as fraud: A verified legitimate change can proceed through the proper process.
