# M7 content script

## SL55: Pause, verify, report, recover
Time: 16:40–16:43 (3 minutes). Layout: response. Evidence: guidance.
Objective: Remember actionable steps

Screen copy:
- Pause the sensitive action.
- Verify through an established route.
- Report uncertainty or a mistaken action.
- Follow recovery instructions.

Evidence to create: Course mnemonic.
Interaction: Ask audience to name an example for each.
Reveal: No hidden answer.
Facilitator notes: Label this our course mnemonic, not a NIST framework.
Sources: S20

## SL56: Your actual reporting route
Time: 16:43–16:48 (5 minutes). Layout: report_demo. Evidence: guidance.
Objective: Demonstrate the customer process

Screen copy:
- [CONFIRMED REPORTING ROUTE]
- Tell us what happened and when.

Evidence to create: Customer screenshot or clearly labelled demo workflow.
Interaction: Trainer demonstrates actual route if confirmed, otherwise generic sequence with visible placeholder.
Reveal: Preserve original message and describe the action taken.
Facilitator notes: Use customer-config values. Never invent an address/button or send a report during training.
Sources: S20

## SL57: What happened determines the response
Time: 16:48–16:51 (3 minutes). Layout: response. Evidence: guidance.
Objective: Distinguish mistakes and ownership

Screen copy:
- Clicked
- Entered credentials or approved access
- Ran software or transferred money

Evidence to create: Action-specific response cards.
Interaction: Match situation to immediate reporting/containment owner.
Reveal: Contact security promptly; bank/finance escalation for payments.
Facilitator notes: Password reset alone may not remove sessions or app permissions. Avoid unplanned wipe/reboot.
Sources: S7, S12

## SL58: A useful report
Time: 16:51–16:53 (2 minutes). Layout: report_demo. Evidence: guidance.
Objective: Provide facts without secrets

Screen copy:
- Time and channel
- Claimed sender and action taken
- Device and original evidence

Evidence to create: Fictional completed report card.
Interaction: Reveal one fact missing from a poor report.
Reveal: Include facts and uncertainty; omit passwords, OTPs and tokens.
Facilitator notes: Do not forward suspicious content widely.
Sources: S20

## SL59: A new request, the same decision
Time: 16:53–16:56 (3 minutes). Layout: decision. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Check learning transfer

Screen copy:
- A genuine HR request asks for sensitive staff information.

Evidence to create: New department and request, same verification principle.
Interaction: Choose approved disclosure route after independent verification.
Reveal: Genuine identity does not replace authorisation.
Facilitator notes: Use ASSESSMENT.md parallel check. No identifying scores.
Sources: S20

## SL60: The check you will use tomorrow
Time: 16:56–16:58 (2 minutes). Layout: reflection. Evidence: guidance.
Objective: Commit to an achievable behaviour

Screen copy:
- Name one sensitive request and your verification route.

Evidence to create: Pairs or table reflection.
Interaction: Each participant gives one concrete action.
Reveal: No hidden answer.
Facilitator notes: Offer a 30-day refresher, not a claim of guaranteed behaviour change.
Sources: S20

## SL61: Questions and references
Time: 16:58–17:00 (2 minutes). Layout: references. Evidence: guidance.
Objective: Close with access to guidance

Screen copy:
- Questions
- Participant response card
- Primary-source reference index

Evidence to create: Expandable sources grouped by subject.
Interaction: Open reference panel; no automatic external navigation.
Reveal: No hidden answer.
Facilitator notes: All cited source IDs resolve. Keep additional questions within allocated time or follow up.
Sources: S18, S20, S21


### SL59 explicit decision key

- Confirm the authorised task and approved sharing route: Share only permitted information through the approved route. [Preferred]
- Report all HR data requests as phishing: Some requests are genuine and still need authorisation checks.
