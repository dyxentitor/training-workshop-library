# M0 content script

## SL01: The request before 5 PM
Time: 10:00–10:02 (2 minutes). Layout: cover. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Establish the fictional cast and baseline decision

Screen copy:
- A familiar name. A payment deadline.
- What would you check?

Evidence to create: Farid receives Invoice MS-1048 at 16:42.
Interaction: Open the first message.
Reveal: No hidden answer.
Facilitator notes: Say Meranti is fictional. Introduce Aina, Farid, Mei and Ravi. Do not disclose the outcome.
Sources: Fictional/logistical course design.

## SL02: A request that fits the job
Time: 10:02–10:06 (4 minutes). Layout: email. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Inspect evidence without assuming trust

Screen copy:
- What can this email actually prove?

Evidence to create: Nadia asks Farid to use changed bank details. Expected .example sender and destination.
Interaction: Expand sender and destination.
Reveal: A matching address does not prove who controls the account.
Facilitator notes: Ask what changed and what must be verified. Use the prototype text.
Sources: S7

## SL03: Another familiar name appears
Time: 10:06–10:09 (3 minutes). Layout: chat. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Recognise pressure from an apparent manager

Screen copy:
- Does this conversation give Farid enough confidence to pay?

Evidence to create: Ravi display name urges payment, then says he is in a meeting.
Interaction: Reveal messages one by one; replay.
Reveal: No hidden answer.
Facilitator notes: Ask what changed in the evidence. A display name is insufficient.
Sources: Fictional/logistical course design.

## SL04: What should Farid do next?
Time: 10:09–10:13 (4 minutes). Layout: decision. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Choose independent verification

Screen copy:
- Choose an action.
- What makes the verification route trustworthy?

Evidence to create: Expected invoice, changed bank details, manager pressure.
Interaction: Vote before selecting.
Reveal: Call the supplier’s established contact and follow normal approval.
Facilitator notes: A genuine request would still need the same process.
Sources: S7

Decision options:
- Pay using revised details: The bank change remains unverified. 
- Reply to Nadia for confirmation: The same mailbox cannot provide an independent check. 
- Call the supplier using existing records: This gives an independent verification route. [Preferred]

## SL05: The callback changes the picture
Time: 10:13–10:17 (4 minutes). Layout: annotated. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Separate original clues from newly verified facts

Screen copy:
- The supplier denies the change.
- Ravi denies sending the chat.
- Farid holds the payment and reports.

Evidence to create: Same email as SL02.
Interaction: Reveal findings.
Reveal: Independent verification supplies new evidence.
Facilitator notes: Do not imply participants should have guessed facts revealed only now. IT investigates account misuse.
Sources: S7

## SL06: When the meeting looked convincing
Time: 10:17–10:20 (3 minutes). Layout: case. Evidence: documented.
Objective: Apply verification to a documented video fraud

Screen copy:
- Approximate reported loss: HK$200m
- Email, prerecorded video and payment messages

Evidence to create: Official January 2024 Hong Kong incident.
Interaction: Ask which independent check could interrupt the sequence.
Reveal: Use an independent authorised transaction workflow.
Facilitator notes: Preserve prerecorded/no-interaction detail. No victim screenshot or invented dialogue.
