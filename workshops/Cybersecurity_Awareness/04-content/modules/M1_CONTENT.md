# M1 content script

## SL07: Impersonation and phishing
Time: 10:20–10:24 (4 minutes). Layout: comparison. Evidence: guidance.
Objective: Distinguish concepts

Screen copy:
- Impersonation: claiming a trusted identity.
- Phishing: persuading you to take a harmful action.
- Spear phishing: targeting a particular person or group.

Evidence to create: One copied profile leads to a document invitation.
Interaction: Ask which concept describes each step.
Reveal: The techniques can overlap.
Facilitator notes: Keep terms plain. Definitions are course paraphrases.
Sources: S5, S10

## SL08: What can someone learn about Aina?
Time: 10:24–10:30 (6 minutes). Layout: profile. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Identify public context used in persuasive messages

Screen copy:
- Job title
- Project announcement
- Supplier and colleague names

Evidence to create: Fictional public profile and event post.
Interaction: Reveal public details; identify one that makes a message credible.
Reveal: Knowing workplace details does not prove employment or authority.
Facilitator notes: Use only invented posts. Explain that public context can reduce suspicion without victim blame.
Sources: S11

## SL09: Two profiles with the same face
Time: 10:30–10:36 (6 minutes). Layout: comparison. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Avoid judging authenticity from copied profile details

Screen copy:
- Which details can be copied?
- How would you contact Aina independently?

Evidence to create: Two fictional profiles with same initials, employer and project details.
Interaction: Compare profiles; select verify rather than guessing.
Reveal: Use the established directory/contact.
Facilitator notes: No score for guessing from account age or follower counts.
Sources: S5

## SL10: A genuine account, an unusual request
Time: 10:36–10:41 (5 minutes). Layout: chat. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Understand compromised account risk

Screen copy:
- The account may be real.
- The request still needs authorisation.

Evidence to create: Known colleague account asks for a staff contact list.
Interaction: Reveal request, then ask how to verify need and permitted disclosure.
Reveal: Follow data-sharing rules and independently verify the purpose.
Facilitator notes: Distinguish account compromise from profile cloning.
Sources: S8, S11

## SL11: The support page that found you
Time: 10:41–10:47 (6 minutes). Layout: browser. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Recognise brand impersonation

Screen copy:
- An unsolicited support contact offers to fix an account issue.

Evidence to create: Search advert/social direct message pointing to fake brand support.
Interaction: Inspect claimed identity and route to known support.
Reveal: Start support from the known service/app.
Facilitator notes: Do not suggest every advert is malicious.
Sources: S5, S6

## SL12: A collaboration that sounds relevant
Time: 10:47–10:54 (7 minutes). Layout: chat. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Reassess a sensitive action after a normal conversation

Screen copy:
- A normal conversation can lead to an unexpected request.

Evidence to create: Contact discusses a project before requesting a document/login.
Interaction: Reveal benign opener then sensitive follow-up.
Reveal: Re-evaluate when money, access or information is requested.
Facilitator notes: Pair exercise: identify the point requiring verification. Keep business names fictional.
Sources: S11

## SL13: Someone is using your name
Time: 10:54–11:00 (6 minutes). Layout: response. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Respond to cloning versus takeover

Screen copy:
- Preserve the profile details.
- Report and warn affected contacts through a trusted channel.
- Use official account recovery if the genuine account is compromised.

Evidence to create: A copied profile sends requests to colleagues.
Interaction: Sort cloning and takeover response cards.
Reveal: Password changes alone do not remove a copied profile.
Facilitator notes: Internal security/communications owns work-related coordination. Avoid promising platform takedown times.
Sources: S5

## SL14: Verify before you classify
Time: 11:00–11:05 (5 minutes). Layout: decision. Evidence: fictional.
Objective: Choose a next action with uncertain identity

Screen copy:
- A profile looks familiar, but the request is new.

Evidence to create: Aina’s apparent profile requests a work document.
Interaction: Choose: trust photo, continue in chat, verify through directory.
Reveal: Verify the request and permission to share.
Facilitator notes: Ask learners to justify the route, not only the classification.
Sources: S5, S20

Decision options:
- Trust the photo: Profile details can be copied. 
- Ask in the same chat: The person controlling that chat can answer. 
- Contact through the staff directory: Verify independently and check sharing permissions. [Preferred]
