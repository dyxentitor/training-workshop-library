# Response and reporting

| Situation | Employee action | Responsible follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Suspicious message, no interaction | Use approved report action; preserve original | Security assesses related activity |
| Opened a link | Stop interacting and report exact action | Security determines whether download/access/execution occurred |
| Entered credentials/approved session | Report immediately through a trusted route | IT/security revokes relevant sessions/tokens and resets credentials as appropriate |
| Granted app permissions/linked device | Report app/device and time | IT removes unauthorised grants/devices and investigates |
| Ran command/software or allowed control | Stop interaction and follow incident procedure; contact IT via another device if needed | Responsible team contains device and preserves evidence; avoid unplanned wipe/reboot |
| Transferred money | Tell finance/security and contact bank through established channel immediately | Attempt recovery and coordinate records/external reports |
| Found copied profile | Preserve URL/handle/timestamps and notify | Communications/security coordinates platform report and contact warning |

Useful report: time/timezone, channel, claimed sender, action taken, device and original message/evidence. Include uncertainty. Never include passwords, OTPs, tokens or unnecessary personal information. Do not forward suspicious content to a wide colleague list.

Customer configuration supplies internal route. External MyCERT/Cyber999 guidance is supplementary; recheck current contact information and customer escalation policy before presenting. Do not embed a foreign authority complaint route as the customer's internal procedure.

Source context: S7 payment response; S12 account/session effects; S20 behavioural programme. This table is a course synthesis, not verbatim official incident instructions.
