Presentation clickers that send Page Up and Page Down work as previous and next. Shortcuts pause while a menu or panel is open and while you are using a control on a slide. Use Tab, Enter and Space to operate buttons as usual. On a phone or tablet, swipe horizontally on empty slide space.

Most slides show everything at once. Pause-point slides (votes, reveals and the tabletop) wait for one click so the room can discuss first. Choices and reveals are kept while you move around the deck and cleared when the page reloads. “Reset this interaction” in the menu restores only the current slide.

Presenter notes are shown on this screen, not privately. They open as a panel over the current slide, so a projected audience sees them. Use the printed facilitator guide for private reference.

Portraits and scene photographs

Character portraits and the desk-scene photographs are AI-generated illustrations of fictional people; no real person is depicted. Diagrams are plain-language course illustrations, not reproductions of vendor figures.
