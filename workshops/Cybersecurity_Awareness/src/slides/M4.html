Grants: access for the sign-in attempt that triggered it, wherever it came from.

“Meeting Helper wants to read your mail and files.”

Grants: the app can use that data, often without asking again.

{{icon:grant-device}}Linked device

“Link a new device to your messaging account.”

Grants: that device can read and send messages as you.

A session, an MFA approval, an app permission or a linked device can each let someone act as you.

  • None of them needs your password to be shared.
  • Check what access you are granting: what does it give? To whom? Did you start it?
