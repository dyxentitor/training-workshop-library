Verify

Verify through an established route.

For example: call the supplier from the record, or the colleague from the directory.

{{icon:report|lg}}

Report

Report uncertainty or a mistaken action.

For example: “I entered a code at 14:05 and I’m not sure the page was ours.”

{{icon:recover|lg}}

Recover

Follow recovery instructions.

For example: sign out where IT asks, and let the responsible team decide next steps.
