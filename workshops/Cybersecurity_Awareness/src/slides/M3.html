SignalWhat it establishesWhat it does not establish
Familiar logo

Someone used that image.

Logos are easy to copy.

Known sender

The message came from, or claims, that address.

Accounts can be compromised; display names can be faked.

HTTPS padlock

Your connection to that site is encrypted.

Who runs the site, or why it asks.

No email warning

Filters did not flag it.

Filters miss new and targeted messages.

Fluent wording

Someone wrote it carefully, possibly with AI help.

Grammar is a weak signal of intent.

MFA completed

Someone passed a sign-in check.

That the business request is valid.

None of these signals validates the request.

  • HTTPS still matters: it protects the connection, not the request.
