Impersonation

Claiming a trusted identity

A person, colleague, supplier or service that you already know.

Phishing

Persuading you to take a harmful action

Pay, sign in, share information, approve access or open something.

Spear phishing

Targeting a particular person or group

The message is shaped around your role, project or contacts.

Identity on a screen is easy to borrow.

  • Verify the person through a route you already trust.
