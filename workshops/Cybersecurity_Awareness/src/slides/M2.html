Three pressure tactics · anyone can feel them

  1. 1Urgency. “Before the 3 PM meeting” leaves little time to check.
  2. 2Secrecy. “Keep it between us” stops others from noticing.
  3. 3An exception to normal approval. “I’ll approve it later” removes the control that protects the payment.

Urgency and authority are reasons to check, not reasons to skip the check.

  • Each tactic tries to remove a check. The risk is the exception, even if the sender is genuine.

Remember

Knowing names does not prove identity.

  • Hang up.
  • Call the service desk using its known number.
  • Never read out a sign-in code.

A caller can learn your name, your manager and your projects from public details. A genuine service desk can wait while you call it back.

Spotting glitches

Odd lip movement, blurred edges, a flat voice

Limit Realistic media may show none of these, and a recording needs no live reaction. Useful to notice, unreliable as the only defence.

Independent verification

Call back on a known number; use the authorised payment workflow

Works either way It does not depend on judging the media. A genuine Ravi can approve through the same process.

A separate, authorised payment process works whether the video is real or fake.

  • Do not rely on spotting glitches.
  • Not every video call is unsafe, and not every fake is detectable.
