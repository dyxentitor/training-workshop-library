# Your workplace response card

Pause the sensitive action. Verify through a route you already trust. Report uncertainty or a mistake promptly. Follow recovery instructions from the responsible team.

Before sharing information, sending money or approving access:
- Identify what the request would disclose or authorise.
- Use the known app, saved address, staff directory or established vendor record.
- Keep the usual approval and data-sharing process.
- Check which session, app or device receives access.

A logo, fluent message, known account or HTTPS alone does not validate a request. A genuine request can still require independent verification.

If you already acted, report what happened and when. Include channel, claimed sender, action and device, with original evidence if available. Do not include passwords or codes. Stop further interaction and follow the incident procedure. Contact finance/bank promptly for a payment.
